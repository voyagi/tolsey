Privacy notice

Tolsey is a demo of a food safety log you fill in by voice. This page says what happens to data when you use it, why, where, for how long, and what you can ask for. Updated 17 October 2026.

Who is responsible

Taranity is the controller. Write to support@taranity.com about anything on this page.

What is processed, and why

Every use below rests on GDPR Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interests. Nothing asks for consent, and there are no accounts.

What Why, and the interest it serves Where How long
IP addresses of visitors Cloudflare needs them to route and protect each request. The interest: delivering and protecting each request. Only in transit, through Cloudflare's network Never stored by Tolsey and never written into its log lines. A log line Cloudflare keeps is dropped after 3 days.
What you write in the demo: readings, refusals, questions and their answers To show a spoken reading becoming a record line. The interest: showing the product to a visitor who chose to try it. The initials and the words sent with each reading To show who logged a reading and what was heard, for the same interest Stored with the line they belong to As long as that line
Screen and request ids To answer a retried call once, with the same line, for the same interest Stored with the line they belong to As long as that line
Your theme choice: Light, Dark or Device To show the page the way you picked Only in this browser. It is never sent to Tolsey. Until you clear it from the browser

The words come in one way only: typed into the field or picked from a phrase on the wall. Nothing listens to a microphone and no voice is recorded. The initials come from a closed roster of invented initials, and any other initials are refused. Only the words Tolsey recognises are kept (a check point, the number, a few state words); anything else you type is dropped before it is stored.

Log lines

The Worker writes a log line only when something fails, with the error's name and code, never what was sent. Cloudflare keeps that line for 3 days and stores it with details of the request: its method, URL and path, the request, ray and trace ids, the Worker's name and version, the account id and a time. A line read after the first deploy held no IP address, request header or body. Cloudflare hides a scratch kitchen's id in the URL by a rule it calls a heuristic, so a line may still hold that id for those 3 days. Cloudflare's per-request logs and traces are switched off.

Where, and the transfer to the US

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts this page, the Worker and the database, and is the only service Tolsey sends anything to. There is no analytics. Cloudflare acts as processor under its Customer DPA, version 6.4. The database is created in EU regions, but a request can be handled in a Cloudflare data centre outside the EU, and Cloudflare, Inc. is in the US. That transfer is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (clause 6.2(a) of the DPA), and in clause 6.4 Cloudflare states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

What you can ask for

You can ask for access to your data, its rectification, restriction of processing, its portability or its erasure, and you can object to its processing. To ask for deletion, or for any of these, write to support@taranity.com.

Nothing Tolsey stores identifies you: there are no accounts, names or contact details (GDPR Article 11). So a request can be met only for data you can point to, which is a scratch kitchen's id: the 32 characters after /mcp/k/ in the requests your browser sent. Every scratch kitchen is deleted within 24 hours in any case.

You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens: https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/en/submitting-a-tip-off-or-a-complaint-to-the-ap.

No automated decisions

No automated decision is made about a person. A verdict judges a temperature against its rule, never the cook who logged it.

In this browser

This browser keeps only your theme choice, under the name tolsey-theme. There are no cookies, and nothing else is stored here.

Accessibility

Tolsey aims to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA on this page and on the wall, checked in a browser with axe-core and by keyboard before each release. If anything here is hard to use, write to support@taranity.com and say where.
